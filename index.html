How to Fight

Every gym counts punches the same way. When Pop yells "one-two-three," he means jab, cross, hook. Keys 1–4 throw them too.

1JabLeft clickFast, cheap, sets everything up.

Hold Shift to throw any punch to the body. Body shots sneak under a high guard and drain his stamina. Chain punches quickly for combo damage; named combinations like 1-2-3 pay a bonus.

Watch his shoulders: a straight pulls back, a hook swings wide, an uppercut dips low. His glove glints as he loads up. Block just before impact to parry. Land while he's winding up, or after he misses, for a counter. Throwing the same punch over and over teaches him to see it coming.

Three knockdowns ends it. If you go down, mash to beat the count.
